VisibilityOS saw brand presence change in 82 of 817 matched AI answer comparisons. The overall rate was 10.0%. Here’s how the counts and rates varied across the eight engine views.

What the study observed

An answer counted as present if it named the brand or linked to its official site. A pair changed when that combined label flipped.

What to report in your next audit

Report both the count and the rate for each engine and setting. Keep question text, model, search settings, language, and market aligned between rounds. When the same questions repeat across days, keep the question IDs so you can account for repeated observations in later analysis.
