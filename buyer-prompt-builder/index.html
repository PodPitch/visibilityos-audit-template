Buyer Prompt Builder

Turn a buying situation into five questions you can ask again under the same conditions. Keep open-ended discovery separate from questions that name your product.

Describe the buyer

The starting values use a fictional product. Leave your product name out of the first three fields.

Your prompt set

Open-ended discovery

  1. Shortlist

    What customer support software should a 12-person support team shortlist?

  2. Use case

    Which customer support software would help a 12-person support team route incoming requests to the right specialist?

  3. Buying criteria

    How should a 12-person support team compare customer support software options when they need to route incoming requests to the right specialist?

Named product checks

  1. Product identity

    What is Larkfield in the customer support software category? Cite the official product site if you can verify it.

  2. Capability

    Can Larkfield help a 12-person support team route incoming requests to the right specialist? Cite the exact source for your answer.

Run a comparable second check

  1. Save the exact questions before the first run. Ask each question in a fresh conversation.
  2. Keep the wording, assistant, model, search setting, language, and region consistent when you repeat the set.
  3. For discovery, count whether the assistant volunteered your product name. For named checks, score whether it described the correct product and linked to evidence.
