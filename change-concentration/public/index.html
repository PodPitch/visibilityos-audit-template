Where did AI brand presence change?

In our study, brand presence changed in 82 of 817 matched comparisons. See what each AI engine view contributed to the sample and to the changes we observed.

Grok accounted for 9.9% of the comparisons and 25.6% of the changes. It contributed 21 changes across 81 repeated checks.

How to read these shares

Divide a view’s comparison count by 817 for the first share. Divide its change count by 82 for the second. A change means the tracked brand appeared or was linked in just one of the two matched answers.

These shares describe this study’s questions, dates, and configurations. The full study reports each view’s own change rate and explains how comparisons were matched.
