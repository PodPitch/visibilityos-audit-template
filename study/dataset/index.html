Field definitions

engine_view
The engine and mode in the published comparison table.
comparisons
The number of matched pairs for the engine view or pooled measure.
presence_changes
Pairs where the tracked brand was present on one date and absent on the other. A mention or link counts as presence.
measure and count
The pooled outcome and its count. The gains, losses, and unchanged counts add to 817. The link-status measure can overlap any of those outcomes.

How the data was collected

VisibilityOS matched observations with the same question, brand configuration, engine view, recorded model, measurement mode, country, language, and location. It kept the last eligible observation for each UTC date and paired consecutive eligible dates.

The files contain study totals only. The sample uses monitored questions about PodPitch-owned websites. Questions and observations can contribute to more than one pair, and coverage varies by engine view.

See the VisibilityOS guide to comparing the same engine mix for a worked example of how coverage differences can change a pooled rate.
